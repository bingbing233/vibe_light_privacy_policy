Privacy Policy

This app respects and protects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Permissions Used

  • Camera: Used to take photos and provide camera-related features within the app.
  • Storage: Used to save photos and access images stored on your device. All files are stored locally and are not uploaded to any server.
  • Foreground Service: Used to update the floating window's feature like lightness and opacity while the app is in use.
  • Display over other apps (Overlay): Used to display a floating light or camera assist feature on top of other applications.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Data Storage

All data (such as photos) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
